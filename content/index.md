# Welcome to [[Nate]]'s website!

> [!info] What is this?
> This will be something of a digital garden—my corner of the internet where I can publish notes and other thoughts. I host it myself on a [Raspberry Pi 3 B](https://www.raspberrypi.com/products/raspberry-pi-3-model-b-plus/) that I've had for years. Check out the source here: https://github.com/cakeebot/nates-website
